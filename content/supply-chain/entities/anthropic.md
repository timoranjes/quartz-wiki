# Anthropic

Anthropic is an AI safety research company and developer of the Claude large language model series. As of May/June 2026, it raised $65 billion in funding at a post-money valuation of $965 billion, overtaking OpenAI in valuation and approaching the $1 trillion mark.

## Funding & Valuation

- **May 2026:** Raised $65 billion, post-money valuation $965B
- Notable investors include [[micron]], [[samsung]], and [[sk-hynix]] — the three major HBM suppliers all took equity stakes
- This marks the first time upstream memory chip suppliers have invested directly in a downstream AI lab
- Valuation overtook OpenAI's, positioning Anthropic as the most highly-valued private AI company
- **June 2026:** Filed confidentially for IPO with the SEC — Claude maker races OpenAI and SpaceX to become the first mega-cap AI lab to go public

## Supply Chain Significance

The participation of memory suppliers as equity investors represents a structural shift in the AI supply chain:

- **HBM demand security:** [[sk-hynix]], [[samsung]], and [[micron]] collectively control ~100% of the HBM market. Their equity stakes in Anthropic create a direct link between compute demand (AI labs) and supply (memory manufacturers)
- **Vertical integration signal:** Memory suppliers are transitioning from pure component vendors to strategic partners with skin in the game at the application layer
- **Competitive positioning:** Each supplier likely secured preferential HBM allocation terms as part of their investment, further entrenching the supply chain dynamics around the largest AI labs

## Claude Ecosystem & Concept Stocks

Chinese market analysis has identified a "Claude concept stock" ecosystem (Claude概念股) — companies positioned across the equity, compute, and enterprise distribution chains that benefit from Anthropic's growth. This mirrors the "NVIDIA concept stock" pattern where upstream suppliers and downstream integrators ride the momentum of dominant AI platforms.

## Competitive Context

- Claude competes directly with OpenAI's GPT series, Google's Gemini, and emerging Chinese models
- The $965B valuation signals market confidence in Anthropic's ability to capture a significant share of the AI model market
- Hyperscaler demand for Claude API access drives downstream compute infrastructure investment

## Relationships

- HBM suppliers as investors: [[micron]], [[samsung]], [[sk-hynix]]
- Competes with OpenAI for AI model market leadership
- Major consumer of AI compute infrastructure (data centers, GPUs, networking)
