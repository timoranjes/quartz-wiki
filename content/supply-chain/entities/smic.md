# SMIC (Semiconductor Manufacturing International Corporation)

**Type:** entity
**Tags:** #semiconductor #foundry #china #smic

## Overview
SMIC is China's largest semiconductor foundry, headquartered in Shanghai. It is the key player in China's domestic chip manufacturing capabilities.

## Key Facts
- **Founded:** 2000
- **Headquarters:** Shanghai, China
- **Technology Node:** 7nm (N+2) in production, working on more advanced nodes
- **Role:** China's primary domestic alternative to TSMC
- **US Sanctions:** Subject to US export controls restricting access to advanced equipment (ASML EUV, Applied Materials)

## Supply Chain Impact
- Central to China's semiconductor self-sufficiency strategy
- Limited by US sanctions on advanced lithography equipment
- Mature node capacity (28nm+) expanding rapidly
- Key supplier for domestic Chinese chip design companies
