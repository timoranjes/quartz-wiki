# Xcena

Xcena is a South Korean memory chip startup that raised $135 million in funding (June 2026) at a $570 million valuation to develop its MX1 memory chip.

## Market Position

- **Valuation:** $570 million (June 2026)
- **Funding:** $135 million raised
- **Location:** South Korea
- **Product:** MX1 memory chip

## Significance

- Part of the broader memory innovation wave driven by AI compute demand
- Entering a market dominated by [[sk-hynix]], [[samsung]], and [[micron]]
- Represents growing investor interest in memory alternatives as HBM supply remains tight
