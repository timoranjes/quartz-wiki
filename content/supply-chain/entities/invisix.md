# Invisix

Invisix is a spin-out from [[asml]] that raised €20 million (June 2026) to develop laser-based chip X-ray inspection technology for semiconductor manufacturing.

## Overview

- **Founded:** Spun out from ASML
- **Funding:** €20 million raised (June 2026)
- **Technology:** X-ray inspection/ metrology for semiconductor manufacturing using lasers
- **Focus:** Quality control and yield improvement for advanced node chips

## Significance

- ASML spin-outs typically carry deep expertise in semiconductor metrology and inspection
- X-ray inspection is critical for advanced packaging (CoWoS, chiplets) where internal defect detection is essential
- Complements ASML's core lithography business by addressing post-lithography quality control
- Part of the broader trend of specialized metrology tools needed for sub-3nm nodes
