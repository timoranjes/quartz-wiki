# 智维创芯 (Zhiwei Chuangxin)

AI-powered EDA (Electronic Design Automation) startup focused on chip design verification automation.

## Overview

- Founded 2025, spun out of the National EDA Innovation Center (EDA国创中心)
- Addresses the chip design verification bottleneck: verification typically consumes >60% of chip development time (architecture to tape-out takes ~2 years); first-time tape-out success rate for high-complexity chips is only 14% (per Siemens/Wilson Research Group 2024)
- **Angel round:** tens of millions RMB, led by 国中资本, with 石溪资本 and 奇绩创坛 (Luqi奇绩) participating

## Core Product: ChatDV

- Industry's first LLM-agent product for digital chip verification (launched May 2025)
- Covers test generation, assertion generation, reference model construction, and auto-debugging
- **Performance claims:** 10x+ chip development efficiency improvement, 50% cycle reduction, 33% cost reduction
- Four modules:
  - **iTest:** Auto-generates TestBench and test stimuli — for ~5,000-line RTL modules, reduces 1.5 person-months to 10 minutes (with sufficient GPU compute)
  - **iSVA:** Auto-generates SystemVerilog assertions (SVA) — reduces complex assertion development from 3 days to hours
  - **iModel:** Auto-generates Golden Model for functional comparison — 1.69-4.89x pass rate improvement vs. general SOTA LLMs
  - **iDebug:** Auto-locates and fixes errors from simulation logs — 89% fix rate

## Technical Approach

- "AI for EDA" route: unlike general LLMs that only do language/code generation, ChatDV combines LLMs with domain-specific tool chains and continuous interaction with chip verification tools
- Data moat: chip verification hardware code, test platforms, and assertions are mostly closed-source within companies; internet-available data is sparse and low-quality (mostly teaching-level simple code)
- Training pipeline: LLMs + other toolchains generate and annotate massive high-quality data → sustainable data flywheel → automated verification flow without manual line-by-line review

## Supply Chain Significance

- Chip design verification is a critical bottleneck in the semiconductor supply chain — delays here cascade downstream to foundry scheduling, packaging, and time-to-market
- AI-powered EDA tools could compress chip development cycles, reducing the time from architecture to tape-out and increasing effective foundry throughput
- Particularly relevant for China's semiconductor localization push: faster design cycles help domestic fabless companies iterate more quickly on alternative architectures in response to export controls
