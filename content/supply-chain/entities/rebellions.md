# Rebellions

Rebellions is a South Korean AI chip startup pursuing a memory-centric architecture for AI inference, differentiating from conventional GPU-centric designs.

## Architecture Strategy

- **Memory-centric design:** Prioritizes memory bandwidth and capacity as the primary compute bottleneck, rather than raw GPU compute
- Partnering with [[sk-hynix]] and [[samsung]] — the two dominant Korean memory makers — to co-design chips that exploit next-generation memory technologies
- This approach aligns with the industry trend toward tighter GPU-memory co-design (see [[hbm-memory]] evolution)

## IPO Ambitions

- Weighing IPO options as of June 2026
- Memory-centric positioning differentiates Rebellions from GPU-centric competitors (NVIDIA, AMD)
- Korean government support for domestic AI chip ecosystem provides strategic backing

## Supply Chain Significance

- Represents an emerging class of AI chip startups that design around memory bottlenecks rather than compute bottlenecks
- Partnership with SK Hynix and Samsung creates a Korean AI chip + memory ecosystem that could challenge NVIDIA's dominance in inference workloads
- If successful, validates the thesis that memory architecture (not just GPU compute) is the key constraint for AI inference — reinforcing [[hbm-memory]] and [[ai-supply-chain-bottlenecks]] analysis

## Relationships

- Partnering with [[sk-hynix]] and [[samsung]] for memory-centric chip co-design
- Competing in AI inference accelerator market against [[nvidia]] and other GPU/ASIC makers
