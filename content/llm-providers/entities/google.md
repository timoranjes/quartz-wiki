# Google (DeepMind / Google AI)

## Overview
Google's AI research is conducted through Google DeepMind and Google AI divisions. Major contributor to transformer architecture and large-scale AI research.

## Key Models
- **Gemini 1.5 Pro**: Long-context multimodal model (up to 2M tokens)
- **Gemini 1.5 Flash**: Fast, efficient model for everyday tasks
- **Gemini 1.0 Ultra**: Most capable model in the Gemini family
- **PaLM 2**: Previous generation large language model

## Products
- **Gemini**: Consumer and enterprise AI assistant
- **Vertex AI**: Cloud platform for model deployment
- **Google AI Studio**: Developer experimentation platform
- **Bard**: Conversational AI interface (now transitioning to Gemini branding)
