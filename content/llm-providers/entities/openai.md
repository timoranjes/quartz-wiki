# OpenAI

## Overview
OpenAI is a leading AI research and deployment company based in San Francisco, founded in 2015. Known for developing the GPT series of large language models and ChatGPT.

## Key Models
- **GPT-4 family**: Multimodal models with vision and text capabilities
- **GPT-3.5 Turbo**: Cost-effective model for general tasks
- **DALL-E**: Image generation models
- **Whisper**: Speech recognition and transcription
- **Embeddings**: Text embedding models for semantic search

## Products
- **ChatGPT**: Consumer and enterprise chat interface
- **API**: Developer platform for model access
- **ChatGPT Enterprise**: Business-focused deployment with enhanced security
