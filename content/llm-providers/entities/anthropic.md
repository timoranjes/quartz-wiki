# Anthropic

## Overview
Anthropic is an AI safety and research company founded in 2021, focused on building reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. Headquartered in San Francisco.

## Key Models
- **Claude 3.5 Sonnet**: High-performance model for complex reasoning
- **Claude 3.5 Haiku**: Fast, cost-effective model for everyday tasks
- **Claude 3 Opus**: Most capable model for advanced analysis
- **Claude 3 Sonnet**: Balanced performance and speed

## Products
- **Claude.ai**: Consumer chat interface
- **Claude API**: Developer platform
- **Claude Enterprise**: Business deployment with enhanced features
- **Claude Code**: Developer-focused coding assistant
