# Stability AI

## Overview
Stability AI is primarily known for **image generation** (Stable Diffusion) but also develops language models (Stable LM).

## Key Models (2026)

| Model | Type | Notes |
|-------|------|-------|
| Stable Diffusion 3/4 | Image generation | Industry-leading open model |
| Stable Audio | Audio generation | Music/sound effects |
| Stable LM 3 | Language | Open-weight, 3B–7B range |
| Aurora | Image | Next-gen image model |

## Strengths
- **Image generation**: Market leader in open-weight image models
- **Multimodal**: Expanding from image → audio → language
- **Open-weight philosophy**: Most models released with open weights

## Positioning
Primarily an image/audio company. Language models are secondary to their diffusion model business.
