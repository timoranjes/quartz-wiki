# Apple

## Overview
Apple develops on-device AI models optimized for **Apple Silicon** (M-series, A-series chips).

## Key Models (2026)

| Model | Params | Notes |
|-------|--------|-------|
| OpenELM | 270M–3B | Open-weight, research-focused |
| Ajax | — | Internal foundation model |
| Apple Intelligence models | — | On-device Siri, writing tools |

## Strengths
- **On-device**: Models optimized for Apple Silicon
- **Privacy**: All processing on device
- **Integration**: Deep integration with iOS/macOS
- **Open-weight**: OpenELM available for research

## Positioning
Hardware-integrated AI. Apple doesn't compete on cloud API benchmarks — their strategy is on-device intelligence.
