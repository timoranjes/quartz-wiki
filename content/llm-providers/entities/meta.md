# Meta (FAIR)

## Overview
Meta's Fundamental AI Research (FAIR) lab develops open-source large language models under the Llama brand. Headquartered in Menlo Park, California.

## Key Models
- **Llama 3.1 405B**: Largest open-source model, competitive with closed models
- **Llama 3.1 70B**: High-performance mid-size model
- **Llama 3.1 8B**: Efficient model for edge deployment
- **Code Llama**: Specialized for code generation
- **Llama 2**: Previous generation (still widely used)

## Products
- **Llama models**: Open-weight models for research and commercial use
- **Meta AI**: Consumer AI assistant integrated into Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp
- **Llama on cloud platforms**: Available through AWS, Azure, GCP
