# 01.AI (零一万物)

## Overview
01.AI, founded by Kai-Fu Lee, is a leading Chinese AI lab behind the **Yi** series of models. Known for strong bilingual (Chinese/English) capabilities.

## Strengths
- **Bilingual**: Strong Chinese and English performance
- **Open-weight**: Yi-1.5 series available for self-hosting
- **Research backing**: Strong academic and research team
- **Cost efficiency**: Competitive pricing vs. western providers

One of China's top AI labs, competing with Alibaba, DeepSeek, and Zhipu. Strong focus on open-weight releases alongside proprietary models.
