# Huawei (盘古 / Pangu)

## Overview
Huawei's **Pangu** models are part of their full-stack AI strategy, running on Huawei's own Ascend (昇腾) chips.

## Key Models (2026)

| Model | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| Pangu 5.0 | Latest flagship |
| Pangu NLP | Language-specific |
| Pangu Vision | Image understanding |
| Pangu Weather | Weather forecasting (specialized) |
| Pangu Drug | Drug discovery (specialized) |

## Strengths
- **Full-stack**: Own chips (Ascend), own framework (MindSpore), own models
- **Industry-specific**: Domain models for weather, drug discovery, etc.
- **Sovereignty**: Fully Chinese tech stack — important for government/enterprise

## Positioning
Full-stack AI strategy with domestic chips. Important for China's AI sovereignty narrative. Less competitive on consumer benchmarks but strong in specialized industry applications.
