# Nous Research

## Overview
Nous Research is an open-weight AI research lab known for the **Hermes** series of instruction-tuned models.

## Key Models (2026)

| Model | Base | Notes |
|-------|------|-------|
| Hermes 3 | Llama 3.1 | Leading instruction-tuned open model |
| Hermes 2 | Various | Previous generation |

## Strengths
- **Instruction tuning**: Hermes models are among the best instruction-following open models
- **Open-weight**: All models freely available
- **Community**: Strong open-source community

## Positioning
Research lab focused on improving open-weight models through instruction tuning. Not a foundation model trainer — enhances existing models.
