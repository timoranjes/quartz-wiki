# Baidu (文心 / ERNIE)

## Overview
Baidu's **ERNIE** (Enhanced Representation through kNowledge Integration) is one of China's earliest and most established LLM products.

## Key Models (2026)

| Model | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| ERNIE 4.5 | Latest flagship |
| ERNIE Bot (文心一言) | Consumer-facing chatbot |
| ERNIE Speed | Cost-optimized variant |
| ERNIE Lite | Edge-optimized variant |

## Strengths
- **Search integration**: Baidu Search grounding
- **Enterprise**: Baidu Cloud integration
- **Early mover**: One of China's first major LLM products
- **Knowledge graph**: Strong entity/knowledge integration

## Positioning
China's earliest major LLM product, but facing stiff competition from newer labs (DeepSeek, Qwen, Zhipu). Strong in enterprise but losing ground on model quality.
