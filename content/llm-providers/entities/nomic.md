# Nomic

## Overview
Nomic specializes in **embedding models** and data visualization. Their Atlas platform enables exploratory analysis of large text datasets.

## Key Models (2026)

| Model | Type | Notes |
|-------|------|-------|
| Nomic Embed | Embedding | 8192 context, competitive with text-embedding-3 |
| Atlas | Visualization | Large-scale data exploration |

## Strengths
- **Embeddings**: Industry-leading open embedding models
- **Visualization**: Atlas platform for data exploration
- **Open-source**: All models and tools open-source

## Positioning
Embedding and visualization specialist. Not a general LLM provider — focused on the embedding layer of the AI stack.
