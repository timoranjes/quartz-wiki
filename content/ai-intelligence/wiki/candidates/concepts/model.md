- title: Knowledge Distillation tags: training, optimization # Knowledge Distillation ## Overview Knowledge distillation trains a smaller "student" model to replicate the behavior of a larger "teacher" model. [source:knowledge-distillation-12873957]
- This transfers frontier-level reasoning capabilities into cheaper, faster, deployable models. [source:knowledge-distillation-12873957]
- ## Distillation Approaches ### Output Logit Distillation (KL Divergence) - Student minimizes KL divergence from teacher's output distribution - Captures teacher's uncertainty, not just top-1 predictions - Most common approach for model compression ### Codistillation (Meta Llama 4) - Teacher model (Behemoth, ~2T params, unreleased) provides training signal during student pre-training - Not post-training distillation — teacher signal integrated into pre-training loss - Llama 4 Maverick (400B) and Scout (109B) both codistilled from Behemoth - Advantage : Student learns teacher's knowledge distribution from the start, not as a retrofit ### Synthetic Data Distillation (DeepSeek R1) - DeepSeek R1 generates reasoning traces for math/coding problems - Smaller models trained on these synthetic traces - Achieves reasoning capability transfer without human annotation - R1 distills into open-weight V3 and V4 families ### Nemotron Distillation (NVIDIA) - NVIDIA's Nemotron models use distillation for enterprise deployment - Specialized variants for code, reasoning, and dialogue distilled from larger base models ### Phi Distillation (Microsoft) - Phi-4 trained on synthetic textbook-quality data - Synthetic data generated by larger models, filtered for quality - Achieves 70B-class performance with 3.8B parameters ## Provider Distillation Pipelines | Teacher Model | Student Models | Method | Quality Retention | |--------------|----------------|--------|------------------| | Behemoth ~2T (Meta) | Llama 4 Maverick 400B, Scout 109B | Codistillation | ~85-90% on MMLU | | GPT-5.5 (OpenAI) | GPT-5.4, GPT-o3-mini | Output distillation | Proprietary | | DeepSeek V4 Pro | V4 Flash, V3.2 | Synthetic data + DPO | ~90% coding, ~85% reasoning | | NVIDIA Nemotron-5 | Nematron-5-22B, 8B | Logit distillation | Model-dependent | | Microsoft Orca 2 | Phi-3.5, Phi-4 | Synthetic textbook data | 70B-class at 3.8B | ## Distillation for Reasoning The "R1 distillation" trend (pioneered by DeepSeek) involves: 1.
