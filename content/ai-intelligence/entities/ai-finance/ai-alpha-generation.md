# AI for Alpha Generation

## Overview

AI-driven alpha generation uses machine learning models — particularly LLMs and deep learning — to identify market inefficiencies, predict price movements, and generate trading signals that outperform benchmarks.

## Approaches

### LLM-Based Signal Generation
- Earnings call transcript analysis for sentiment shifts
- News aggregation and event impact scoring
- SEC filing analysis for early warning signals
- Social media sentiment (Reddit, X, StockTwits)

### Traditional ML Alpha
- Factor models enhanced with ML (random forests, gradient boosting)
- Alternative data processing (satellite imagery, credit card data)
- Cross-asset correlation discovery
- Regime detection and adaptive strategies

### Deep Learning
- LSTM/Transformer-based time series forecasting
- Reinforcement learning for portfolio optimization
- Graph neural networks for supply chain contagion
- Attention-based multi-modal fusion

| Firm | Approach | Notable |

## LLM-Specific Opportunities

1. **Unstructured data advantage** — LLMs process text that traditional quant models cannot
2. **Cross-lingual signals** — Chinese social sentiment, European regulatory filings
3. **Reasoning chains** — Models can explain why a signal exists, not just that it exists
4. **Rapid iteration** — Prompt-based strategy testing vs. full code rewrites

## Risks

- **Signal decay** — Alpha from public LLMs degrades as more participants use them
- **Overfitting** — LLM hallucination can look like pattern recognition
- **Regulatory scrutiny** — AI-generated trading signals face increasing compliance requirements
