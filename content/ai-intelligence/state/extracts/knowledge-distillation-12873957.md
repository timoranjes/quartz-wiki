# Knowledge Distillation

## Overview

Knowledge distillation trains a smaller "student" model to replicate the behavior of a larger "teacher" model. This transfers frontier-level reasoning capabilities into cheaper, faster, deployable models.

## Distillation Approaches

### Output Logit Distillation (KL Divergence)
- Student minimizes KL divergence from teacher's output distribution
- Captures teacher's uncertainty, not just top-1 predictions
- Most common approach for model compression

### Codistillation (Meta Llama 4)
- Teacher model (Behemoth, ~2T params, unreleased) provides training signal **during** student pre-training
- Not post-training distillation — teacher signal integrated into pre-training loss
- Llama 4 Maverick (400B) and Scout (109B) both codistilled from Behemoth
- **Advantage**: Student learns teacher's knowledge distribution from the start, not as a retrofit

### Synthetic Data Distillation (DeepSeek R1)
- DeepSeek R1 generates reasoning traces for math/coding problems
- Smaller models trained on these synthetic traces
- Achieves reasoning capability transfer without human annotation
- R1 distills into open-weight V3 and V4 families

### Nemotron Distillation (NVIDIA)
- NVIDIA's Nemotron models use distillation for enterprise deployment
- Specialized variants for code, reasoning, and dialogue distilled from larger base models

### Phi Distillation (Microsoft)
- Phi-4 trained on synthetic textbook-quality data
- Synthetic data generated by larger models, filtered for quality
- Achieves 70B-class performance with 3.8B parameters

## Provider Distillation Pipelines

| Teacher Model | Student Models | Method | Quality Retention |

| Behemoth ~2T (Meta) | Llama 4 Maverick 400B, Scout 109B | Codistillation | ~85-90% on MMLU |
| GPT-5.5 (OpenAI) | GPT-5.4, GPT-o3-mini | Output distillation | Proprietary |

## Distillation for Reasoning

The "R1 distillation" trend (pioneered by DeepSeek) involves:
1. Large reasoning model generates CoT traces for problems
2. Filter and clean the traces
3. Train smaller models on these traces
4. Result: smaller models with reasoning capabilities

This has been replicated by:
- Qwen distilling reasoning into Qwen3.6-3B variants
- DeepSeek R1 distills available as open weights
- Multiple providers releasing R1-inspired distilled models

## Trade-offs

- **Capability ceiling**: Student cannot exceed teacher's capabilities
- **Bias amplification**: Student inherits teacher's biases and limitations
- **Task specialization**: Distilled models may overfit to teacher's training distribution
- **Compute savings**: Distilled models can be 10-100× cheaper to serve
