# SpaceX TeraFab

**Type:** entity
**Tags:** #semiconductor #fab #spacex #tesla #intel #xAI

## Overview
TeraFab is a planned semiconductor fabrication plant jointly developed by Tesla, SpaceX, xAI, and Intel. Announced by Elon Musk on March 21, 2025.

## Key Facts
- **Investment:** $55 billion initial, up to $119 billion total
- **Location:** Grimes County, Texas (near College Station)
- **Tax Abatement:** 35-year, 100% property tax exemption approved June 2026
  - SpaceX pays $10M upfront + $20M/year to county
  - Despite resident backlash and threatened legal action
- **Purpose:** Supply AI/semiconductor chips for SpaceX, xAI, and Tesla
- **Intel Partnership:** Intel joined as partner for manufacturing expertise

## Timeline
- **2025-03-21:** Announced by Elon Musk
- **2026-05-07:** Property tax abatement agreement sought
- **2026-06-03:** Grimes County approved tax incentives (Reuters)
- **2026-06-04:** FT confirmed property tax exemption won

## Supply Chain Impact
- Vertical integration play for AI compute hardware
- Reduces dependence on TSMC/Samsung for custom AI chips
- Signals major capex shift from cloud providers to in-house semiconductor manufacturing
- Property tax abatement model may attract other chip projects to Texas
