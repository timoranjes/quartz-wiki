# MiniMax: Comprehensive Research Report (2026)

## Executive Summary

MiniMax Group (MiniMax AI, Chinese: 稀宇科技, *Xīyǔ Kējì*) is a Shanghai-based artificial intelligence company that has emerged as one of China's leading AI startups, often classified among the "AI Tigers" alongside Moonshot AI (Kimi), DeepSeek, and Zhipu AI. Founded in December 2021 by former SenseTime researchers, MiniMax has built a multimodal AI platform spanning text, audio, image, video, and music generation. The company completed a landmark Hong Kong Stock Exchange IPO in January 2026, raising approximately HK$4.8 billion (~$619 million), and has become one of the most well-capitalized Chinese AI labs.

As of mid-2026, MiniMax's flagship models include the M2 series (M2, M2.1, M2.5, M2.7) and the newly released M3.0 (June 2026) with 1M token context window and native multimodality. M2.7, released March 18, 2026, features a revolutionary self-evolving architecture, competitive agentic capabilities, and industry-leading cost efficiency. MiniMax's approach combines frontier-level performance with aggressive pricing, positioning its models at a fraction of the cost of Western alternatives like Claude Opus and GPT-5.

The company operates a global platform at minimax.io with over 200 million users, offering both API access through platform.minimax.io and consumer-facing products including Talkie (character AI) and Hailuo AI (video generation).

## Company Overview

### Founding and Leadership

MiniMax was founded in December 2021 by Yan Junjie (严俊杰), a former Vice President at SenseTime with over 30,000 academic citations, along with co-founders Yang Bin and Zhou Yucong, all from SenseTime's computer vision research division. Yan, born in 1990, is recognized as one of China's youngest AI entrepreneurs and became a billionaire following MiniMax's Hong Kong IPO in January 2026, with a personal net worth estimated at $3.2 billion.

The founding team's SenseTime background provided deep expertise in computer vision, machine learning, and large-scale model training, which MiniMax has leveraged to build a comprehensive multimodal AI platform. Yan Junjie was reportedly once dismissed as a "fraud" by big tech early in his career, according to a Bloomberg Q&A in January 2026, making his subsequent success particularly notable.

## Technical Architecture

### Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) Design

MiniMax's latest models use sparse MoE architecture:

- **Total Parameters**: Significantly larger than active count
- **Active Parameters**: ~10B per token
- **Expert Routing**: Intelligent activation of relevant experts per token
- **Efficiency**: Compute cost similar to dense 10B model with much larger capacity

### Self-Evolving Framework (M2.7)

The key innovation in M2.7 is its self-evolving architecture:

1. **Automated Scaffold Optimization**: The model iteratively improves its own prompt templates, tool configurations, and workflow patterns
2. **RL Research Automation**: Autonomously manages portions of the reinforcement learning pipeline
3. **Competition-Driven Improvement**: Participates in ML competitions as a mechanism for self-assessment and improvement
4. **Continuous Learning Loop**: Breaks the traditional train-eval-deploy cycle with ongoing self-improvement

### OpenClaw Agent Harness

M2.7 is built using the OpenClaw framework:

- **Agent Architecture**: Structured multi-agent collaboration
- **Scaffold Optimization**: Automated prompt and workflow optimization
- **Tool Integration**: Native tool use and function calling
- **Role Management**: Defined agent roles and communication protocols

### Self-Evolution Mechanism

The self-evolving architecture operates through several mechanisms:

1. **Scaffold Iteration**: The model generates and tests improved prompt templates
2. **Performance Feedback**: Benchmark results inform optimization direction
3. **Autonomous RL**: The model manages portions of its own reinforcement learning
4. **Competition Participation**: ML competitions provide structured evaluation

### Agent Architecture

M2.7 features native multi-agent collaboration:

- **Role Boundaries**: Defined roles for different agents in a team
- **Adversarial Reasoning**: Agents can challenge and critique each other's outputs
- **Protocol Adherence**: Structured communication between agents
- **Active Decision-Making**: Beyond response generation to autonomous action

- **Video Generation**: Text-to-video capabilities (Hailuo Video-01, Hailuo 2.3)
