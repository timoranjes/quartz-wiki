# Helion Energy

**Type:** entity
**Tags:** #fusion-energy #data-center #microsoft #altman #clean-energy

## Overview
Helion Energy is a fusion energy startup building the world's first commercial fusion power plant. Backed by Sam Altman, with a power purchase agreement to supply Microsoft by 2028.

## Key Facts
- **Headquarters:** Everett, Washington
- **Latest Round:** Series G — $465M (June 2026)
- **Lead Investor:** Thrive Capital
- **Valuation:** $15.5B post-money (up from $5.4B in Jan 2025 Series F)
- **Microsoft Deal:** Power purchase agreement for commercial fusion power by 2028
- **Goal:** Commercialize fusion power this decade

## Supply Chain Impact
- Fusion power could solve data center energy constraints
- Microsoft's PPA validates commercial viability timeline
- Relevant to AI infrastructure: compute growth limited by power availability
- If successful, removes a key bottleneck for hyperscale AI deployment
