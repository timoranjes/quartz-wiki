# StepFun: Comprehensive Research Report (2026)

## Executive Summary

StepFun (阶跃星辰) is a Shanghai-based artificial intelligence startup founded in April 2023 by **Jiang Daxin**, former Microsoft executive and Chief Scientist of Microsoft Software Technology Center Asia. The company specializes in **multimodal AI foundation models** capable of processing and generating text, audio, image, and video content, and has rapidly emerged as one of China's leading AI startups.

StepFun is a member of China's prestigious **"Six AI Tigers"** (大模型六小虎) — the collective designation for the six most prominent Chinese AI startups building large language models to compete globally. The company achieved unicorn status in December 2024 with a reported $2 billion valuation and has raised over **$718 million** in total funding, including significant backing from Shanghai state-owned capital.

As of mid-2026, StepFun has launched over 20 models, with its **Step-3.5-Flash** (February 2026) and **Step-3.7-Flash** (May 2026) models achieving top rankings on key benchmarks. The company is actively planning a **Hong Kong IPO** with a target valuation of $10 billion, having begun unwinding its offshore incorporation structure in April 2026 to comply with Chinese regulatory requirements.

#### Architecture Philosophy: "Small Sparse"

StepFun's approach flips the scaling-law narrative:

| Camp | Example | Active Params | Key Insight |
|------|---------|---------------|-------------|
| Dense Giants | GPT-5.4, Claude Opus 4.7 | >500B | High quality, high cost |
| Sparse Behemoths | Kimi K2.6 (1T), DeepSeek V3.2 (671B) | 32–37B | Balance of quality & efficiency |
| **Small Sparse** | **Step-3.5-Flash (196B)** | **11B** | **Best cost/perf for math/code** |

**Why it wins**:
1. Curated training data (Phi-style quality focus)
2. Efficient expert routing
3. Agent-first training objective (tool use & multi-step coherence baked in)
4. 11B active params can be served from a single H100 with good throughput

#### Key Innovations

**Three Reasoning Depths**:
- **Low**: Fastest, cheapest — simple completions
- **Medium**: Balanced cost and reasoning depth
- **High**: More compute per response — complex agent tasks

**Advisor Mode**:
- StepFun's implementation of Anthropic's advisor strategy
- Runs full agentic loop (tool use, iteration, planning)
- Escalates to larger model only at inflection points
- Most of the run stays at executor (Flash) cost

### Python SDK

```python
from openai import OpenAI

client = OpenAI(
    api_key="YOUR_STEPFUN_API_KEY",
    base_url="https://platform.stepfun.com/v1"
)

# Chat completion with reasoning level
response = client.chat.completions.create(
    model="step-3.7-flash",
    messages=[
        {"role": "system", "content": "You are a helpful assistant."},
        {"role": "user", "content": "Explain the small sparse MoE architecture"}
    ],
    max_tokens=2048,
    temperature=0.7,
    reasoning_level="medium"
)

print(response.choices[0].message.content)

# Vision-language
response = client.chat.completions.create(
    model="step-3.7-flash",
    messages=[
        {
            "role": "user",
            "content": [
                {"type": "text", "text": "Describe this image in detail"},
                {"type": "image_url", "image_url": {"url": "https://example.com/photo.jpg"}}
            ]
        }
    ],
    max_tokens=1024
)

# Streaming
stream = client.chat.completions.create(
    model="step-3.5-flash",
    messages=[{"role": "user", "content": "Write a story about AI"}],
    stream=True
)

for chunk in stream:
    if chunk.choices[0].delta.content:
        print(chunk.choices[0].delta.content, end="")

# Advisor mode for agentic tasks
response = client.chat.completions.create(
    model="step-3.7-flash",
    messages=[
        {"role": "user", "content": "Debug and fix this Python script: ..."}
    ],
    advisor_mode=True,
    max_tokens=4096
)
```

### Prompt Engineering Guide

**StepFun-Specific Best Practices:**

1. **Reasoning Levels**: Use `low` for fast responses, `medium` for balanced, `high` for complex tasks
2. **Advisor Mode**: Enable for agentic workflows (auto-escalates to larger models when needed)
3. **Context Window**: Leverage 256K tokens for long document processing
4. **Tool Definitions**: Provide clear JSON schemas for function calling
5. **Few-Shot**: 2-3 examples improve quality on specialized tasks
