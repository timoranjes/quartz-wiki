# Google (Gemini)

## Company Facts
- Founded: October 2, 2015 (Alphabet Inc., restructuring of Google)
- HQ: Mountain View, CA, USA (Googleplex)
- CEO: Sundar Pichai (CEO of both Google and Alphabet)
- Funding/Valuation: Public company (GOOGL/GOOG), ~$3T+ market cap (Sep 2025 reached $3T milestone)
- Employees: ~194,668 (Mar 2026)

## Capabilities
- Reasoning: yes (Deep Think enhanced reasoning mode on 2.5 Pro)
- Vision: yes (multimodal input: text, image, video, audio)
- Audio: yes (Flash Live real-time audio-to-audio, TTS, STT)
- Tool Use: yes (grounding with Google Search and Maps)
- Agentic: yes (Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform)
- Coding: strong

## Key Facts
- Google DeepMind, led by Demis Hassabis, develops Gemini models alongside Google AI.
- Gemini 3.1 Pro (GA Feb 19, 2026) supports up to 2M token context window.
- Google announced $80B equity raise (Jun 2026, incl. $10B from Berkshire Hathaway) to fund AI infrastructure.
- Revenue for Alphabet: $402.8B (2025), net income $132.2B.
- Free tier (as of Apr 1, 2026) limited to Flash/Flash-Lite models; Pro models now paid-only.
- Gemini 2.0-flash and 2.0-flash-lite were deprecated June 1, 2026; migration path to Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite.
