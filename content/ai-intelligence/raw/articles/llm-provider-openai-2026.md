## Key Facts
- Founded as a nonprofit in 2015; transitioned to a for-profit Public Benefit Corporation (PBC) in October 2025.
- Largest private tech fundraise in history: $122B in committed capital (Feb 2026) at $852B post-money valuation.
- Revenue grew from $3.7B (2024) to $13.1B (2025); projected net loss of ~$9B in 2025 due to high inference costs ($8.4B).
- Major partnerships include Microsoft ($13B+ invested, 27% stake), Apple (ChatGPT integration in Apple Intelligence), Oracle ($300B cloud deal), and NVIDIA ($100B MOU).
- Key products: ChatGPT (100M+ users in 2 months after launch), GPT-5.5, Sora (text-to-video), DALL·E (image generation), Codex (code generation, basis for GitHub Copilot), Operator (web navigation agent), Deep Research (research agent).
