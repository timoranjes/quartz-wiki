## Capabilities
- **Multi-Model Platform**: Together AI hosts models from Qwen, DeepSeek, MiniMax, Meta (Llama), Google (Gemma), and many others
- **Serverless Inference**: Chat, Vision, Image, Audio, Video, Transcribe, Embeddings, Rerank, Moderation
- **Fine-Tuning**: Custom model fine-tuning on Together AI's GPU infrastructure
- **Dedicated Endpoints**: Single-tenant GPU instances for guaranteed performance
- **GPU Clusters**: Access to GPU clusters for large-scale training/inference
- **Image Generation**: FLUX, Wan, Qwen Image, Google Imagen, Stable Diffusion, and more
- **Video Generation**: Qwen3.6-Plus, Google Veo, Kling, Wan, Sora 2, MiniMax Hailuo
- **Audio**: TTS (Cartesia Sonic, Orpheus, Kokoro), transcription (Whisper, Parakeet)
- **Embeddings**: Multilingual e5 large instruct at $0.02/1M tokens
- **Moderation**: Llama Guard 4 12B at $0.20/1M tokens

## Key Facts
1. Together AI was founded in 2022 by Chris Ré (Stanford), Ce Zhang, Percy Liang (Stanford), Vipul Ved Prakash, and Tri Dao (FlashAttention creator).
2. Based in San Francisco — it operates as an AI-native cloud providing inference, fine-tuning, and GPU compute.
3. Raised $305M Series B led by General Catalyst; total funding ~$554M+ with ~$1B raised at $7.5B valuation.
4. Estimated $1B in annualized revenue as of February 2026 (Sacra).
5. Together AI is a model-agnostic platform, not a model builder — it aggregates and serves models from multiple providers.
6. Offers Batch Inference API at 50% lower cost for most models, making it cost-effective for large-scale processing.
