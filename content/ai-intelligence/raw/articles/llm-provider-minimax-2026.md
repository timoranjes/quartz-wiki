## Capabilities
- **Reasoning**: M3 designed for agentic reasoning; M2.7 introduced recursive self-improvement
- **Coding**: Strong programming benchmarks (M2.1 focused on "Polyglot Programming Mastery")
- **Tool Use**: Full tool calling and agentic workflows
- **Vision**: Multimodal chat input (M3)
- **Audio**: Speech-2.8 TTS with lifelike voice cloning; rapid voice cloning ($1.5/voice)
- **Video**: Hailuo 2.3/2.3-Fast video generation up to 1080p, 10s
- **Music**: Music-2.6 generates up to 5-minute songs ($0.15)
- **Long Context**: M3 supports up to 512K+ tokens (limited access)

## Key Facts
1. MiniMax was founded in 2021 by Dr. Yan Junjie, former VP of SenseTime, and is headquartered in Shanghai.
2. The company went public in Hong Kong in January 2026, achieving a market cap over $40B (WSJ).
3. MiniMax released M3 (June 2026) as their latest model for agentic reasoning, tool use, coding, and multimodal chat.
4. M2.7 (March 2026) marked the beginning of "recursive self-improvement" in their model lineage.
5. MiniMax has diversified beyond LLMs into audio (Speech), music (Music-2.6), video (Hailuo), and image generation.
