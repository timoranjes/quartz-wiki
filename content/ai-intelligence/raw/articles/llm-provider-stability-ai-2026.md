# Stability AI

## Company Facts
- **Founded**: 2020
- **HQ**: London, United Kingdom
- **CEO**: Christian Laforte
- **Core Business**: Open-source generative AI models for images, video, audio, 3D, and language

## Capabilities
- **Brand Studio** (launched April 2026): Enterprise creative production platform for on-brand image generation and editing with sequential workflows
- **Stable Audio 3.0** (May 2026): Open-weight audio generation models for artistic experimentation
- **Self-Hosted Deployment**: Full data control and customization via license
- **API Access**: Production-ready tools via Stability AI Platform
- **Enterprise Features**: Indemnification, expert support, compliance-ready integration

## Key Facts
1. Stability AI pioneered open-source image generation with Stable Diffusion (2022), a diffusion-based text-to-image model.
2. Stable Diffusion 3.5 (October 2024) remains the flagship open-weight image generation family with Large, Large Turbo, and Medium variants.
3. Brand Studio launched in April 2026 as an enterprise managed creative platform targeting marketing, gaming, and entertainment workflows.
4. Enterprise customers include Electronic Arts (EA), Universal Music Group (UMG), HubSpot, ARM, AWS, and NVIDIA.
5. Stability AI offers deployment via self-hosting, API, cloud service, or managed applications (Brand Studio).
6. Core Models list last updated May 20, 2026; models available under Stability AI Community or Enterprise agreements.
