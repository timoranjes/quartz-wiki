# Meta (Llama)

## Company Facts
- Founded: February 4, 2004 (Meta Platforms, Inc.)
- HQ: Menlo Park, CA, USA
- CEO: Mark Zuckerberg
- Funding/Valuation: Public company (META), ~$1.8T market cap (2026)
- Employees: ~78,000 (2026, before 10% layoffs announced)

## Capabilities
- Reasoning: yes (enhanced in Llama 3.3, Llama 4)
- Vision: yes (Llama 3.2+, Llama 4)
- Audio: partial (Llama 4 planned; Ray-Ban Meta glasses use custom Llama)
- Tool Use: yes (Llama Stack for agentic applications)
- Agentic: yes (Llama Stack enables agentic AI)
- Coding: moderate (Code Llama variants available; Llama 4 improved)

## Key Facts
- Llama models have been downloaded over 650 million times (doubled in 3 months as of Dec 2024), averaging 1M+ downloads per day.
- Over 85,000 Llama derivatives published on Hugging Face (5× increase since early 2024).
- Meta AI assistant on track for ~600M monthly active users, available in 43 countries and 12+ languages.
- Llama 4 (Apr 2025) introduced MoE architecture with multimodal capabilities: Scout (109B params, 10M context) and Maverick (400B params, 1M context). Behemoth (~2T params) was not released.
- In April 2026, Meta Superintelligence Labs released Muse Spark as a replacement for Llama.
- Meta raised 2026 AI capital expenditure guidance to $125–145B (up from $72.2B in 2025).
- Llama's license is source-available, not open-source — disputed by OSI and FSF.
