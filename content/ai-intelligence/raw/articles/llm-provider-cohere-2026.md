## Capabilities
- **Reasoning**: Command A+ is Cohere's "fastest and most powerful language model yet"
- **Tool Use**: Command R+ series optimized for tool use and RAG workflows
- **RAG**: Built-in RAG capabilities; Rerank models (3.5, 4 Fast, 4 Pro) for retrieval augmentation
- **Embeddings**: Embed 4 for semantic search and document representation
- **Multilingual**: Aya Expanse models (8B, 32B) for multilingual research applications
- **Enterprise Platform**: North (AI platform for enterprise automation) and Compass (intelligent search & discovery)
- **Model Vault**: Fully managed platform for running Cohere models at scale with guaranteed performance

## Key Facts
1. Cohere was founded in 2019 by Aidan Gomez (CEO), Nick Frosst, and Ivan Zhang. Gomez was a co-author of the "Attention Is All You Need" paper.
2. Based in Toronto, Canada — Canada's leading enterprise AI company.
3. Raised $500M in August 2025 at a $6.8B valuation, accelerating global expansion for enterprise and sovereign AI.
4. Command A+ is Cohere's newest and most powerful model, available as open-source.
5. Cohere focuses on enterprise-grade AI with custom deployments, private deployments, and model customization.
6. Model Vault provides fixed or flex pricing with no multi-tenancy overhead for enterprise customers.
