## Capabilities
- Reasoning: yes (extended thinking mode available on all current models)
- Vision: yes
- Audio: no
- Tool Use: yes (MCP connector, tool use)
- Agentic: yes (Claude Code CLI, Claude Cowork GUI)
- Coding: strong (Opus 4.8 scores 69.2% on SWE-bench Pro)

## Key Facts
- Founded by former OpenAI researchers (Dario Amodei, Daniela Amodei, Jared Kaplan, Jack Clark, Chris Olah, Ben Mann, Sam McCandlish, Tom Brown) in January 2021.
- Structured as a Public Benefit Corporation (PBC), legally bound to pursue public good alongside profit.
- Revenue grew from $10M (2022) to $2.2B (2025), representing 220× growth in three years.
- Major investors: Amazon ($12.5B+, AWS as primary cloud), Google ($2B+, 1M+ TPUs deal), Microsoft ($30B Azure compute deal), Coatue, GIC, Sequoia.
- Uses Constitutional AI (CAI) — training models to adhere to a set of principles without relying solely on human feedback.
- Claude Code reached general availability in May 2025; acquired Bun (JS runtime) in Dec 2025 for speed/stability.
