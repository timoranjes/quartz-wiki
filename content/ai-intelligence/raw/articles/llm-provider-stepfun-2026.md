## Key Facts
1. StepFun (Shanghai Jieyue Xingchen Intelligent Technology) was founded in April 2023 by former Microsoft VP Jiang Daxin.
2. One of China's "AI Tiger" startups alongside Moonshot AI, MiniMax, and others.
3. Raised $2.5B in funding and planned a Hong Kong IPO in June 2026 at a $10B valuation (LinkedIn/Reuters).
4. Step-3.5-Flash (January 2026) is their most capable open-source foundation model with 256K context.
5. StepDeepResearch system released on GitHub in February 2026 for research-oriented tasks.
